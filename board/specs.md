# FoveaCam Duo Driver (FoveaCamDuo-PCB): board specification, V0.1

State 2026-10-07, v0.1.2 (frozen 2026-10-07, tag v0.1.2). Generated by `board/layout.py` from `netlist.py`, `placement.py`,
`routes.py`, `silk_front.py`, `silk_back.py` and `lib/rules.py`. V0.0 is the
hand-drawn board of git 289fb6c. Requirements and the dated decision log are in the
root `specs.md`; this file holds the board's numbers. Unknowns are marked TODO.

## 1. Purpose

A Teensy 4.0 carrier that drives two Mirrorcle MEMS drivers (digital input,
AD5664R), one independent SPI bus each, triggers and reads three FLIR Blackfly S
cameras (four lines each), offers two UARTs and three 5 V fan outputs. No active
parts besides the Teensy; four optional capacitor footprints.

## 2. Block diagram

```
           J_UART1 (PH5)       J_PWR3 (PH2 fan)       J_UART2 (PH5)
           5 3 G RX1 TX1         +5V GND               5 3 G RX2 TX2
                 \                   |                    /
 J_PWR1 (PH2 fan)  J_SPI1 (DRV1) == bus 1 ==  U1 Teensy 4.0 (back) == bus 2 == J_SPI2 (DRV2)  J_PWR2 (PH2 fan)
                     EN, FCLK shared by both ports
                 /                   |                    \
           J_CAM1 (PH6)        J_CAM0 (PH6)          J_CAM2 (PH6)
           L3 IN L2 OUT GND    L3 IN L2 OUT GND      L3 IN L2 OUT GND
```

Power: +5V is the Teensy's VIN (USB VBUS through the Teensy's VIN-VUSB trace);
+3V3 is its 3V3 pin. The 2-pin sockets are fan OUTPUTS (section 5).

Peripheral sources: Teensyduino 1.159 `FlexIO_t4.cpp` (Teensy 4.0 section:
flex1_hardware pins 2, 3, 4, 5 = FlexIO1 D04, D05, D06, D08; flex2_hardware pins 6-13
= FlexIO2 D10, D17, D16, D11, D00, D02, D01, D03), `core_pins.h` (pin 6 =
GPIO_B0_10, pin 2 = GPIO_EMC_04). LPSPI4 (11/12/13) is not used: pin 13 is on the
other row. Every edge GPIO is used, so MEMS_EN stays shared; an enable per driver is
wanted when a later revision frees a GPIO (owner 2026-10-07).

Routing (all checked on every build):

- Each bus's CS / SCLK / MOSI run on F.Cu only, over the unbroken B.Cu ground plane,
  with no crossing and no plane slot under them. DRV1: lanes under the Teensy
  (CS1 -2.54, FCLK 0, SCLK1 2.54, MOSI1 4.6 mm), funnel to J_SPI1, SCLK1 / MOSI1 wrap
  round its south end. DRV2: SCLK2 / MOSI2 straight up from pins 12 / 11 into the
  north band and round the north end of J_SPI2; FCLK / CS2 / MEMS_EN in lanes under
  the Teensy and up risers.
- Shared lines: the J_SPI pin order (FCLK between SYN and SCK, EN north of SYN on
  J_SPI1 but south of it on J_SPI2, the port rotated 180 degrees) forces MEMS_EN to
  cross FCLK once; MEMS_EN (static) takes that crossing on B.Cu. FCLK's north band
  passes over three short B.Cu jumps (MEMS_EN, RX2, TX2), flanked by stitching vias.
  MEMS_EN reaches J_SPI1 from the north and jumps under RX1 / TX1 on B.Cu.
- Lengths: CS1 24.2, SCLK1 38.7, MOSI1 48.4, CS2 38.3, SCLK2 18.6, MOSI2 26.6 mm;
  FCLK tree 86.6, MEMS_EN tree 85.3 mm.
- Spacing: SCLK1 / MOSI1 run 14.1 mm side by side at >= 1.1 mm (edge gap 0.85 mm,
  3.3 W); SCLK2 / MOSI2 14.6 mm at 1.05 mm (3.1 W; V0.1 draft: 12.3 mm at 0.8 mm,
  cross-check 2026-10-07). FCLK beside SCLK1 11.4 mm at 1.27 mm (the descent between
  Teensy pins 2 and 3), beside SCLK2 5.4 mm at 1.15 mm, with GND pour between.
- Track width 0.254 mm on 1.51 mm FR-4 over the B plane: microstrip about 130 ohm
  without the pour, about 100 ohm with the coplanar GND pour 0.2 mm away (estimates;
  no stackup in the board file); delay about 5.8 ps/mm (eps_eff about 3.0).
- Crosstalk on the board, worst case FCLK -> SCLK1 (11.4 mm at 1.27 mm): backward
  coefficient about 0.15 x (2 x 11.4 mm x 5.8 ps/mm) / t_r, so about 1 % of 3.3 V
  (30 mV) at a 2 ns edge: far below the 0.4 V input low limit (Mirrorcle p14). The
  ribbon cable dominates: in the 2x5 IDC cable SCK (pin 8) lies next to SYN (7) and
  FCLK_X (9) for 152 mm with one ground (pin 4); Mirrorcle p10 warns of crosstalk from
  fast FCLK / SPI edges and asks for reduced slew rates.

Timing (SCLK at f, 24-bit frame; board + cable delay about 0.2 ns + 0.8 ns one way):

| SCLK | half period | board SCLK/MOSI skew | frame (24 bits) | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 MHz | 500 ns | < 0.06 ns | 24 us | 96 us |
| 5 MHz | 100 ns | < 0.06 ns | 4.8 us | 19.2 us |
| 10 MHz (recommended maximum until measured) | 50 ns | < 0.06 ns | 2.4 us | 9.6 us |
| 20 MHz | 25 ns | < 0.06 ns | 1.2 us | 4.8 us |

Freeze analysis ("it randomly enters undeterministic state and froze itself"):

- Board (V0.0): one SCLK / MOSI branched to two connectors and two 6 inch cables
  with no termination and the Teensy's strongest drive: reflections from the open
  branch can double-clock the AD5664R; a corrupted 24-bit word can set a channel to
  power-down, switch the reference off or load a wrong code, and the DAC stays there
  until re-initialised. V0.1: one point-to-point bus per DAC, unbroken plane, GND pin
  of each port tied to the plane, stitching along every lane.
- Board / power: the drivers need VDD 4.85-5.2 V (p14) and have supply monitoring
  with auto-shutdown (p3); USB allows 4.75 V at the port, less after the cable and
  the Teensy. A dip (driver start-up pulses of 100 mA, fans) can shut a driver down,
  which looks like a freeze. Measure VDD at J_SPI pin 3 under load (open item).
- Firmware: no FCLK means fixed outputs (p50); CS asserted before the SPI settings
  and broadcast writes on the shared bus (V0.0 code); EN raised before the DAC init
  and FCLK (p27 order); the DAC not re-initialised per power-up (p26). Fixes in
  `board/pins.h` firmware notes and section 6.

## 5. Power and parts

- +5V source: the Teensy's USB only (owner 2026-10-07). Fan budget: USB 2.0 500 mA
  (USB 3.x 900 mA) minus Teensy about 100 mA (TODO measure) minus two drivers 2 x
  38 mA (p14; 100 mA pulses each at start-up) leaves about 300 mA for fans (about
  700 mA on USB 3.x): about 100 mA per fan socket including start-up surge on USB 2.0.
  The Teensy's VIN and VUSB are joined by a cuttable trace; do not feed power into
  the fan sockets.
- Optional capacitors (VARIANT=caps; DNP by default), one type (owner 2026-10-07):

| Ref | Value / part | Position (mm, back) | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 22 uF 25 V X7R 1210, TDK C3225X7R1E226M250AB (Digi-Key 445-C3225X7R1E226M250ABCT-ND; Murata GRM32ER71E226KE15L failed the stock rule 2026-10-07) | (-6.75, -15.5), between J_UART1 and J_PWR3 | J_PWR3 |
| C2 | same | (6.75, -15.5), between J_PWR3 and J_UART2 | north run junction |
| C3 | same | (-27.75, 2.4), between J_SPI1 and J_PWR1 | J_PWR1, J_SPI1 |
| C4 | same | (27.75, 2.4), between J_SPI2 and J_PWR2 | J_PWR2, J_SPI2 |

  Rationale: fans are noisy loads on the rail the drivers use; a 22 uF MLCC next to
  each fan output takes the start-up surge and commutation noise. One type is enough:
  a 1210 MLCC has about 1 nH ESL, so its impedance stays below 0.1 ohm from about
  70 kHz to about 15 MHz; a 100 nF part would only help above about 20 MHz, where the
  track inductance to the fan dominates anyway. DC bias at 5 V leaves roughly
  12-16 uF per part (TODO: read TDK's DC-bias curve). All four exceed the USB 2.0 inrush
  limit of 10 uF (USB 2.0 7.2.4.1), so they stay unfitted unless fan noise needs them.
  Mirrorcle recommends >= 100 uF at VDD in OEM use (p14): that belongs on the driver
  side. Mirror pairs about x = 0, clear of silk, >= pad + 4 mm from every screw pad,
  >= 1.2 mm from the outline (asserted).

## 6. Firmware notes (also in `board/pins.h`)

- Pads (IOMUXC SW_PAD_CTL): SCLKn / MOSIn / CSn DSE 1-3 (150-50 ohm, source
  termination for the cable), SRE 0 (slow), SPEED 0; FCLK DSE 1, SRE 0 (Mirrorcle p10;
  i.MX RT1060 reference manual, SW_PAD_CTL fields).
- AD5664R: 24-bit frames, data on the falling SCLK edge (SPI mode 1 or 2), SYNC low
  for the whole frame; init per power-up 0x280001, 0x380001, 0x20000F, 0x300000 (p26);
  check Vref 1.25 V when debugging (p26).
- Order (p27): DAC init, Vbias on all channels, FCLK running (60 x the cut-off,
  3 kHz-3 MHz, p14 / p16), then MEMS_EN high; shut down in reverse (p31).
- MEMS_EN (pin 6) LOW first thing in setup(). Line3 pins (5, 16, 20) INPUT from boot;
  drive only after the camera's Line3 is set as an input.

## 7. Silkscreen

- Side cue (owner 2026-10-07: "Do not use text to hint sides, use visual cues
  instead"). Visual language: a corner bracket (two 1.0 mm legs, 0.15 mm stroke,
  0.45 mm outside the housing) means "a housing sits here, on this face". Every
  connector housing is framed by brackets on the back only, at each body corner that
  falls on the board, clears other parts, keeps 0.15 mm from pads and vias; a corner
  left out also drops its mirror image, so the marks stay symmetric. The front has no
  brackets. The Teensy, which may go on either face, is marked by a USB icon at its
  USB end on both faces. Removed: the V0.1-draft legends "solder side · parts on the
  back" (front) and "parts side" (back). Variants: SIDE_MARKS=brackets (chosen) or
  icon; renders `build/silk-brackets-*.png`, `build/silk-icon-*.png`.
- Front: connector names, DRV1 / DRV2 (move with the ports), 5V by the fan sockets,
  pin labels (knockout = power: 5 = +5V, 3 = +3V3, G = GND), camera pin numbers.
- Knockout labels (v0.1.1, fab review F6): drawn as filled silk polygons, the glyph
  box grown by KNOCKOUT_MARGIN 0.2 mm with the glyphs cut out (lib/silk.py), so the
  ink round the letters is >= 0.2 mm; KiCad's own knockout leaves max(stroke / 2,
  height / 9) = 0.133 mm, under JLCPCB's 0.15 mm silk minimum. check_dfm `knockout`
  checks the margin (v0.1.0: 20 labels at 0.133 mm).
- UART labels "5 3 G T R" on both faces, centred on the pins, just south of the PH
  housings (owner 2026-10-07); "T" over pin 4 (the RX net) and "R" over pin 5 (the TX
  net) name the cable side's signal. Do not swap them. The centre socket: "5 G".
- Name block unchanged from V0.0 (exempt from the silk-under-body rule).
- Version stamp "V${REVISION} | ${GIT_SHORT}": one centre-justified Roboto Slab bold
  text (1.4 mm), both faces, centred at (0, 10.0), so KiCad centres the whole block
  whatever the variables expand to. Candidates rendered in
  `build/version-font-candidates.png`: Roboto Slab bold (chosen: same family as the
  name block, already vendored and installed by CI, thinnest stroke 0.18 mm >= the
  0.15 mm silk minimum), Roboto Mono bold and Roboto Condensed bold (both thinner,
  0.16 mm, and a second family on the board). Regular weights measure 0.15-0.18 mm,
  too close to the minimum. `build/version-stamp-variants.png`: REVISION 0.1 / 10.12
  x GIT_SHORT DEV / 289FB6C / 289FB6C+, both faces. Inked box >= 1.0 mm from the
  camera pin numbers (asserted); no via or pad under the longest stamp (asserted,
  VERSION_MAX_HALF_W 9.8 mm). TrueType texts are not measured by check_dfm (warning,
  as for the name block); KiCad's text_thickness warning stays on the copyright line
  only.
