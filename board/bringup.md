## 3. Power-up

1. Power: +5V comes only from the Teensy's USB through its VIN pin (VIN and VUSB bridged on the Teensy, as shipped). J_PWR1-3 are 5 V OUTPUTS for cooling fans, never feed power into them (owner 2026-10-07; budget in specs.md 4: about 100 mA per fan on USB 2.0). The optional capacitors (VARIANT=caps) exceed the USB 2.0 inrush limit of 10 uF; fit them only if fan noise needs them.
2. Flash the V0.1 firmware (pins as in board/pins.h, firmware notes at its end) into the Teensy before it goes onto this board: the V0.1 pin map differs from V0.0 on almost every pin.
3. Fit the Teensy (socket strips on the back, USB end west, at the USB icon). Repeat section 1 (+5V to GND and +3V3 to GND must not read as a short).
4. Power up from USB with no MEMS driver, camera or fan connected.
5. Measure +5V (J_UART1 pin 1, J_PWR1-3 pin 1) and +3V3 (J_UART1 pin 2) against GND (J_UART1 pin 3).
6. Check that MEMS_EN (J_SPI1 / J_SPI2 pin 5, Teensy pin 6) reads LOW from reset until the firmware enables the drivers (pin 6 is pulled down during reset and kept by its keeper; the firmware drives it LOW first, pins.h).
7. Load a sketch that toggles each output in board/pins.h and check it at the connector pins of section 2 (MEMS_EN, FCLK, CSn, SCLKn, MOSIn; CAMn_IN); with INPUT_PULLUP on CAMn_OUT and CAMn_L2, shorting the connector pin to GND must read LOW. Leave CAMn_L3 as inputs.
8. Connect one MEMS driver to J_SPI1 (DRV1), check it powers from +5V, then the second on J_SPI2 (DRV2). Bring-up order per the Mirrorcle guide p27: DAC init, Vbias, FCLK, then MEMS_EN high.
9. Connect the cameras (J_CAM0..2): FLIR GPIO cable pin n to J_CAMn pin n. Pin 1 (Line3) is wired to a Teensy pin: never configure the camera to output VAUX (8-24 V) on Line3. Connect opto GND (blue, pin 5) before any signal (FLIR warning).
